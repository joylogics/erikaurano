Erika Urano is a filmmaker, editor, and artist based in New York City. A graduate of NYU's Tisch School of the Arts, where she studied Film and Television with a minor in Creative Writing, she works across writing, directing, and production design, with a focus on character-driven stories and a strong visual language.

She also works as a freelance video editor/content creator, cutting short-form and social content for artists and small businesses. Recent work ranges from event coverage for the API arts nonprofit [Civil Art](https://www.instagram.com/civilart.nyc/) to ongoing social videos for the NYC café Tinto, where her film background shapes even a thirty-second clip.

Raised in New Jersey, she has close ties to both her Japanese and Brazilian heritage and grew up speaking English and Portuguese at home. She has spent two summers in Japan studying language, culture, and art. Beyond film, she writes poetry and nonfiction, and makes fine art with a particular interest in multimedia and surrealist work. Her essay ["The Nose Knows"](https://confluence.gallatin.nyu.edu/context/interdisciplinary-seminar/the-nose-knows) was published in NYU Gallatin's Confluence.
